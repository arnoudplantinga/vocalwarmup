# Piano samples

Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, licensed CC-BY 3.0.
Obtained from https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments (`samples/piano`),
trimmed to 3.2 s with a fade-out and re-encoded to mono MP3 to keep the repo small.

Sampled every three semitones (A2 … C6); other pitches are played by resampling the
nearest neighbour, at most 1.5 semitones away.
